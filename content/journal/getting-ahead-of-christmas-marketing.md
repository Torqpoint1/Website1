Every year it’s the same: the businesses that do well at Christmas are the ones who quietly got ready in October, and the ones scrambling on the first of December are a step behind the whole month. Seasonal marketing rewards the organised, not the energetic. The good news is that a little planning now buys you a calm December later.

## Why ‘now’ is the right time

Customers start thinking about Christmas far earlier than businesses expect. By mid-November, people are booking tables, buying gifts, lining up tradespeople for jobs they want done before the family descends. If your Christmas message only appears in December, you’ve missed the people who planned ahead — which is most of the good ones.

Starting early isn’t about being pushy in October. It’s about having everything *ready* so you can switch it on at the right moment without a panic.

## What to prepare in the next few weeks

- **Your key dates.** Last order dates, last booking dates, opening hours over the holidays, the final day you’ll start new work before the new year. Decide them now.
- **One clear offer or message.** Not five. What’s the one thing you want people to do before Christmas — book, buy, enquire?
- **A simple run of content.** A handful of posts and one email, drafted in advance, scheduled to go out across late November and December.
- **Your shop window — physical and digital.** Update your Google profile hours *before* the holidays, not during them. Nothing annoys a customer like a "closed" sign when you’re open, or vice versa.

## The one email worth sending

For most businesses, a single, well-timed email to past customers in late November does more than a month of frantic posting. "Here are our Christmas dates — book by the 10th to be sure." It reaches the people who already like you, at the moment they’re making plans. That’s the cheapest, warmest marketing you own.

## Don’t forget the quiet bit after

January is when everyone else goes silent. A business that keeps a steady presence through the new-year lull picks up the work that competitors are too hungover to chase. Plan a soft landing into January now, while you’re thinking about it.

## The calm version of December

Picture it: your dates are set, your posts are scheduled, your email is written, and your profile is correct. December arrives and you get to actually run the business, because the marketing is already done. That’s the whole aim.

There’s a difference between someone reading about bathrooms on a Sunday and someone typing "bathroom fitter near me" on a Tuesday morning. The first is curious. The second has a problem and a postcode, and they’re about to call someone. "Near me" searches are the ones worth winning, because the person doing them is ready to act.

## What actually happens in a ‘near me’ search

Type "wedding florist near me" or "barber in Stroud" and Google does something specific: it shows a little map with three business listings pinned to it — the "local pack" — before any normal website results. Most people choose from those three. They rarely scroll past them.

So the real question for a local business isn’t "how do I rank on Google" in the abstract. It’s "how do I become one of those three."

## What decides who gets shown

Google weighs three things, roughly:

- **Relevance** — does your profile clearly say you do the thing being searched?
- **Distance** — how close are you to the person searching?
- **Prominence** — do you look like a real, active, trusted business?

You can’t move your premises, and you can’t argue with distance. But relevance and prominence are both within your control, and most businesses leave them half-finished.

## The practical checklist

1. **Claim and complete your Google Business Profile.** Every field. The category especially — pick the one that matches what people search, not a vague umbrella term.
2. **Say what you do in plain words.** "Wet rooms and accessible bathrooms in Cheltenham" beats "bespoke sanitary solutions."
3. **Keep it active.** Recent photos and the odd post tell Google the business is current. A dormant profile slowly sinks.
4. **Gather reviews, and reply to them.** Volume and recency of reviews are among the clearest prominence signals there are.
5. **Make your address and service area consistent** everywhere it appears online. Mismatches confuse the ranking.

## Why this beats paying for leads

A directory lead is rented and shared. A strong local presence is owned and yours alone. Win the "near me" search and the enquiry comes straight to you — no per-lead fee, no four competitors getting the same call. And unlike an ad, it keeps working the month after you stop thinking about it.

## Where most businesses lose

Not to clever competitors — to their own neglected profile. A half-filled listing with one photo and no recent activity loses to the business down the road that simply keeps theirs switched on.

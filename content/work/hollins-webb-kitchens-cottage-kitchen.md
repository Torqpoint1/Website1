## The job

A cramped 1990s kitchen in a stone cottage just outside Tewkesbury: pine doors, a cracked laminate worktop and a layout that fought the owners every morning. Hollins & Webb stripped it back to the walls, re-ran the first-fix plumbing and electrics, and fitted sage-green shaker cabinets, quartz worktops, oak open shelving and a butler sink under the window. Three weeks on site, snagging done, handed over the Friday before a family Christmas.

## The problem

Hollins & Webb win almost every job where the customer has seen their work in person. The trouble is getting that far. Their enquiries came from a directory, sat in a list next to four other fitters, and were decided on price before anyone saw a finished kitchen.

## What one job became

The fitter took twelve photos across the job — before, at first fix, at handover — and sent a three-minute voice note from the van. That was their whole part. From it:

- **A case study page** for their website, telling the job from brief to handover
- **A before, during and after set**, edited and captioned, for the website and their Google profile
- **Four social posts** — the reveal, the first-fix detail, the butler sink close-up, and the customer's words
- **A Google Business post**, so the job shows up where Tewkesbury searches start
- **A review request**, written for them to send the homeowners on handover day

### Sample — case study opening

> The brief was simple: "we want to be able to cook in it." The room didn't get any bigger. It just stopped getting in the way.

### Sample — Google Business post

> **Just finished: a cottage kitchen near Tewkesbury.** Sage shaker units, quartz tops and a butler sink under the window — stripped back to the stone and rebuilt in three weeks. Before-and-afters on our website. Planning a kitchen? We'll come and measure up.

### Sample — review request

> Thanks so much for having us over the last three weeks — we hope the first proper Sunday roast in the new kitchen goes well. If you've got two minutes, a quick Google review really helps a small firm like ours: [link]. Thanks again — the Hollins & Webb team.

## Why it works

A homeowner spending £15,000 on a kitchen wants to see one finished in detail before they trust anyone with theirs. Now, when Hollins & Webb are asked "can you send some examples?", they send a link — and the conversation stops being about who's cheapest.
